xvi. The collected barren land should be brought for cultivation and  
      traditional plowing activities should be undertaken.

3. Agricultural Engineering Department - General Guidelines:

i.  The village plan implementation committee should consult with the  
    farmers identified in the barren land collection.

ii. Water sources in the barren land collections should be identified, and  
    a project plan should be prepared.

iii. If there is a water source in the area under the barren land collection,  
     a deep well/pond well should be constructed and necessary  
     arrangements for pump sets should be made.

iv. In non-cultivable lands, soil bunds for water conservation should be  
     constructed.

v. Irrigation water sources should be created, ponds and soil bunds  
    should be constructed, and even if there are production units,  
    agricultural activities should be verified and carried out by the  
    agricultural engineer / engineer / accountant engineer.

vi. Further, works should be carried out with the approval of higher  
     officials as per the Tamil Nadu Transparency In Tenders Act, 1998 &  
     Rule 2000, 2012.

vii. During the construction of bund works, various stages should be  
      accounted for by the agricultural engineer / engineer / accountant  
      engineer (Ve.Po.) or by themselves and progress should be  
      documented.

viii. The stages of bund works should be inspected for quality and video  
       documentation should be done.

ix. The Agricultural Engineering Department, Agriculture Department /  
     Horticulture Department should ensure that agricultural activities in  
     barren land collections are approved and inspected by the  
     agricultural engineer (Ve.Po).

x. Details of the work carried out in this collection should be maintained  
    in a register by the agricultural engineer (Ve.Po) or independently.
