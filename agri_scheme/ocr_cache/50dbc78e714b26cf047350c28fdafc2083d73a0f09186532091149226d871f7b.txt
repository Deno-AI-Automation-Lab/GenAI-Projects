ABSTRACT

Agriculture and Farmers Welfare - Horticulture - Centrally Sponsored Scheme of National Horticulture Mission Programme under Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture for 2022-23 - First installment of Rs.2560.000 lakh - Released by Government of India during 2022-2023 - Sanction and release of a sum of Rs.4266.666 lakh including matching State share of Rs.1706.666 lakh as first installment - Orders - Issued.

AGRICULTURE AND FARMERS WELFARE [H1(1)] DEPARTMENT

G.O.(Ms) No.243

Dated : 13.10.2022

திருவள்ளுவர் ஆண்டு 2053
சுபகிருது வருடம்,
புரட்டாசி திங்கள் 26-ஆம் நாள்

Read:

1. G.O.(2D) No.81, Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (AP5) Department, dated 28.04.2022.
2. From the Under Secretary to Government of India, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (Horticulture Division) letter F. No. 33-20/2021-MIDH(AAP), dated 26.05.2022.
3. From the DDO and Under Secretary to Government of India, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Department of Agriculture, & Farmers Welfare, Horticulture Division-MIDH, letter F.No. 18-9/2017-Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (E), dated 28.09.2022.
4. From the Managing Director, Tamil Nadu Horticulture Development Agency, letter No.TAN/NHM/ 3820/2022, dated 07.10.2022.

ORDER:-

In the letter second read above, the Government of India had approved the Annual Action Plan 2022-23 of National Horticulture Mission (NHM) under Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH) for an outlay of Rs.170.796 crore in the funding pattern of 60:40 between the Centre and State and communicated the approved physical and financial targets for each component under Annual Action Plan -2022-23.

2. In the letters third read above, the Government of India have released 1st installment of Rs.2560.000 lakh towards implementation of the National Horticulture Mission as per the approved Annual Action Plan - 2022-23 under Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture.
