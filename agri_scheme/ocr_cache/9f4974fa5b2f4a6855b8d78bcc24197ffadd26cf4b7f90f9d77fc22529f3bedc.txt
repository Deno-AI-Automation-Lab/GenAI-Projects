ORDER:

In the 20th meeting of the State Level Sanctioning Committee (SLSC) for National Agriculture Development Programme (NADP) / Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana (RKVY) held under the Chairmanship of Chief Secretary on 27.05.2022, the SLSC has approved 46 new projects relating to Head of Departments of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare and Cooperation, Food and Consumer Protection Department worth Rs.400.4184 crore under National Agriculture Development Programme for the year 2022-23.

2. In the letter 2nd read above, the Government of India have allocated a sum of Rs.182.32 Crore as Central Share (60% of total allocation) for the year 2022-23 under NADP / RKVY in the funding pattern of 60:40 between Central and the State Governments and approved 50% of the total allocation upto 30.09.2022 as Rs.91.16 crore. By adding the corresponding 40% State share of Rs.60.773 crore, the 50% allocation is worked out as Rs.151.933 crore.
