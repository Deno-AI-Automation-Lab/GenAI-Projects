13. Planning

District collectors must conduct a review meeting every month and send a report on the progress of the plan to the government by the 10th of each month.

A. Technical works carried out through employment status and unasked departments: Guidelines

1. Employment status department - General guidelines

i. Soil testing must be conducted in the identified degraded clusters by the village plan implementation committee.

ii. In the degraded clusters, the selection of sugarcane, banana, and other fruit tree saplings must be done with the farmers' consent.

iii. In the degraded clusters, necessary arrangements must be made for the cultivation of small and medium green plants, and appropriate advice must be given to farmers.

iv. In the degraded clusters with riverbanks, farmer participation must be enhanced in the National Employment Development Plan land measurement plan.

v. The donation fund capital must be divided, and the ministerial donation fund committee must be organized.

vi. In the panchayat union where the plan is implemented, all schemes required for farmers other than the degraded clusters must be integrated and implemented to collect the welfare of other plan beneficiaries.
