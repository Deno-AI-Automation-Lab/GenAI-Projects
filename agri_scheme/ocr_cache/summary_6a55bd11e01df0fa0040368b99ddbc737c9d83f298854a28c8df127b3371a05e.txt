**KEY FACTS - National Horticulture Mission (NHM) - 2022-23**

- **G.O. number and date; issuing department:** G.O.(Ms) No.243, Dated: 13.10.2022; Agriculture and Farmers Welfare Department
- **Scheme name and year; purpose / objective:** National Horticulture Mission (NHM) - 2022-23; Implementation of the National Horticulture Mission under Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture.
- **Total budget / sanction amount and funding share (State / Central):** Total budget of Rs. 4266.666 lakh; Central share of Rs. 2560.000 lakh and State share of Rs. 1706.666 lakh.
- **Components with subsidy pattern, unit cost, rates and limits:**
  - Hi-Tech Nursery - Public: Unit cost Rs. 75.000 lakh, subsidy Rs. 25.000 lakh.
  - Hybrid Vegetables: Unit cost Rs. 3410.000 lakh, subsidy Rs. 2242.597 lakh.
  - Green House structure: Unit cost Rs. 935.000 lakh, subsidy Rs. 214.963 lakh.
  - Adoption of organic farming - III year: Unit cost Rs. 375.000 lakh, subsidy Rs. 375.000 lakh.
- **Eligibility / beneficiaries / priority groups; districts or area covered:** Small and marginal farmers, Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST) categories.
- **Implementing agencies, how to apply, conditions, timelines:** Implemented by Tamil Nadu Horticulture Development Agency; application process details not specified; conditions include adherence to subsidy patterns and project guidelines. Timelines not specified.
