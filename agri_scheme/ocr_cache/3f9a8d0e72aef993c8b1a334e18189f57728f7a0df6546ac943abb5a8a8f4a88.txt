3. Vegetable Seed Inspection Scheme
Brinjal, Okra, Tomato, Broad Beans, Chili, Coriander, Drumstick, Small Onion, Radish, Beetroot vegetable seeds of eight varieties will be provided with a fund of Rs. 0.9612 crore.

i. Under the Green Revolution scheme, 100 seedlings per unit will be provided to 3,20,400 families.
ii. Total price of the individual unit - Rs.40/-
iii. Subsidy - 75 percent (individual unit - Rs.30/-)
iv. Beneficiary's share for one individual unit is Rs.10/-
v. Except for small onion and radish seeds, other vegetable seeds will be produced and distributed from government horticulture farms. Small onion and radish seeds will be procured and distributed from government-affiliated institutions.
vi. Priority will be given to Scheduled Castes and Tribes families.
vii. In all selected sectors, inspection must be certified by regional officers once fully completed.
