**KEY FACTS - State Horticulture Development Scheme (SHDS) 2024-25**

- **G.O. Number and Date; Issuing Department:** G.O. No. M.D.2/4206/2024.1. Date: 20.06.2024; Horticulture and Hill Area Division Department
- **Scheme Name and Year; Purpose / Objective:** State Horticulture Development Scheme 2024-25; To implement the Horticulture Development Scheme and House Improvement.
- **Total Budget / Sanction Amount and Funding Share (State / Central):** Rs. 2,998.072 million; Funding share details not specified.
- **Components with Subsidy Pattern, Unit Cost, Rates and Limits:**
  - Assistance for establishment of infrastructure facilities: Rs. 50,000/Unit
  - Assistance for establishing Moringa Export Zone: Rs. 5,000/Ha
  - Subsidy for organic certification (3 years): Rs. 1.5 Lakhs/Ha
  - Subsidy for organic certification (4th year): Rs. 0.5 Lakhs/Ha
- **Eligibility / Beneficiaries / Priority Groups; Districts or Area Covered:** General (80%), Scheduled Castes (19%), Scheduled Tribes (1%); 2482 villages across various districts.
- **Implementing Agencies, How to Apply, Conditions, Timelines:** District Collectors and officials are responsible for implementation; Application details and specific conditions not provided; Timelines for implementation are for the 2024-25 financial year.
