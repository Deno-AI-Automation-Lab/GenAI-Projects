ENGLISH TRANSLATION:

Operating pump set will be done only up to a depth of 500 feet. For borewells / pipe wells installed beyond 500 feet depth, electric connection pump set will not be provided.

a) Windmill Operated Pump Set Arrangement

i. Windmill operated pump sets with 10 horsepower low electricity fans will be provided for borewells / pipe / open wells up to a depth of 500 feet. In this, borewell safety measures will be implemented for borewells / pipe / open wells. Windmill operated pump sets will be funded from the Chief Minister's Windmill Pump Set Scheme and expenses will be borne by the beneficiaries. For windmill pump sets with 10 horsepower, 30% subsidy certificate, 40% central government funding, and the remaining 30% will be provided by the village panchayat development scheme. In this, safety measures will be funded by the central government and dynamic funding, and the growth mentioned in the March 2020 return will be considered as the basis for development.

ii. Economic justification of the zone will be spent on the protection of central agriculture energy security and development (PM-KUSUM) scheme and the windmill operated pump sets will be funded by the central government and the expenses of the pumps will be borne by the mountain and hill research schemes.

b) Electric Operated Pump Set Arrangement

i. In places where windmill operated pump sets cannot be provided, funding obtained through the Tamil Nadu Electricity Generation and Distribution Corporation (TANGEDCO) will be spent on electric connection through the scheme.

ii. For windmill operated pump sets, electric panel, starter without inspection, necessary safety infrastructure will be increased, and the government will bear the expenses for motors up to 15 HP.

iii. For places where electric motor pump sets are to be installed, the total cost for a motor will be Rs.15,000/- and this amount will be funded by the central government through the zone's scheme.
