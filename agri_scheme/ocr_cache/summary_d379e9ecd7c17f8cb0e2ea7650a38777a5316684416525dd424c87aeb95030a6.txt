### Key Facts - Palmyrah Development Mission (PDM) 2024-25

- **G.O. Number and Date; Issuing Department**:  
  Government Order (Standing) No. 205, Date: 01.09.2022, Agriculture–Farmer Welfare Department [No.Ka.2(1)].

- **Scheme Name and Year; Purpose / Objective**:  
  Palmyrah Development Mission (PDM) 2022-23; to encourage land cultivation and improve the livelihood of farmers.

- **Total Budget / Sanction Amount and Funding Share (State / Central)**:  
  Total budget: Rs. 2.00 crore (Rs. 2,00,00,000).

- **Components with Subsidy Pattern, Unit Cost, Rates and Limits**:  
  - Sale of Palm Seeds: 100% subsidy, target: 10,00,000 seeds, budget: Rs. 30,000.  
  - Award for Discoverers of Superior Palm and Machinery: No subsidy, budget: Rs. 1,000.  
  - Establishment of Units for Producing Palm Products: 75% subsidy, target: 124 units, budget: Rs. 93,000.  
  - Distribution of Equipment for Producing Folded Palm Products: 75% subsidy, target: 510 units, budget: Rs. 30,600.  
  - Distribution of Equipment for Palm Climbing Machines: 75% subsidy, target: 1000 units, budget: Rs. 45,000.  
  - Miscellaneous: No subsidy, budget: Rs. 400.

- **Eligibility / Beneficiaries / Priority Groups; Districts or Area Covered**:  
  Beneficiaries include palm farmers and women involved in palm-related activities; scheme implemented in all districts except Chennai and Nilgiris.

- **Implementing Agencies, How to Apply, Conditions, Timelines**:  
  Implemented by the Department of Horticulture and Hill Crops; applications to be submitted through cooperative societies; conditions include being a member of the Primary Jaggery Co-operative Society and cultivating on own or leased land. Timelines for implementation and application processes are specified in the guidelines.
