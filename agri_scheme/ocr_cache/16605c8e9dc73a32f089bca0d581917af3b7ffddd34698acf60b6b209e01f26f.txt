7.  Procedures for bringing current landholding groups to UP cultivation

i.   Keera Scheme Implementation Group: Consultation should be held 
     with the farmers under the current landholding.
ii.  Soil testing, water testing of the land, and consultation with farmers 
     should be conducted to select suitable crops / saplings for planting.
iii.  Water conservation / water distribution systems should be established 
     in the organized landholding areas of the support / SURAB / 
     permanent group.
iv.  Support well / pond well should be generally established in 
     government land.
v.   Furthermore, if land is not available for well establishment in 
     government UP land, arrangements should be made for the 
     organized farmer associations to acquire the land. These procedures 
     and actions should be carried out by the Keera Scheme 
     Implementation Group. The land of the current landholding group 
     cannot be granted as a patta land to any private (group) farmer. 
     This land should be used for support establishment. For support 
     establishment, a well with operating pump set should be established 
     at a depth of 100 feet (10 feet x 10 feet) or a well with operating 
     pump set in the well wheel.
