**KEY FACTS - National Agriculture Development Programme (NADP) - 2022-23**

- **G.O. number and date; issuing department:** G.O.(2D) No.162, Dated: 12.10.2022; Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (AP1) Department
- **Scheme name and year; purpose / objective:** National Agriculture Development Programme (NADP) for the year 2022-23; to implement project proposals of the Department of Horticulture and Plantation Crops.
- **Total budget / sanction amount and funding share (State / Central):** Total budget of Rs.400.4184 crore; Central Share of Rs.182.32 crore (60% of total allocation) and State Share of Rs.60.773 crore (40% of total allocation).
- **Components with subsidy pattern, unit cost, rates and limits:**
  - Promotion of Crop Diversification Programme: Rs.8,000 per acre.
  - Mission for promotion of Fruit Crops: Rs.20.21 crore for expanding area under fruits to 22,000 acres.
  - Special focus on Garlic Cultivation: Rs.8,000 per acre for 1,250 acres with an outlay of Rs.1 crore.
- **Eligibility / beneficiaries / priority groups; districts or area covered:** Farmers in Tamil Nadu; specific focus on Horticulture and Plantation Crops.
- **Implementing agencies, how to apply, conditions, timelines:** Implemented by the Director of Horticulture and Plantation Crops; applications through the Tamil Nadu Watershed Development Agency; adherence to guidelines and timelines set by the State Level Sanctioning Committee.
