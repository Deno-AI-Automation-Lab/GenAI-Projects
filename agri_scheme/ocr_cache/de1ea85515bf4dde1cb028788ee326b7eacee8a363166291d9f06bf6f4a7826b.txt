4. Expansion of Area for Perennial Horticultural Crops  
Under the expansion of area for perennial horticultural crops,  
cultivation and other inputs will be provided in an area of 10,000 hectares  
with a fund of Rs.18.00 crore.  
i. Necessary cultivation and other inputs will be provided to increase  
the area of horticultural crops like mango, guava, sapota,  
coconut, pomegranate, lemon, tamarind, etc.  
ii. It will be implemented according to the guidelines of the  
Small Horticulture Scheme.  
iii. Total expenditure for one sapota - Rs.60,000/-  
iv. Subsidy - 50% of the total expenditure in the first year, 60%  
of the total expenditure Rs.30,000 sapota will be provided.  
v. Farmers with land in the agricultural land and farmers in the  
selected village groups can benefit.  
vi. Up to 4 hectares can be utilized frequently.  
vii. Priority should be given to small / marginal, industrial,  
Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe farmers.  

5. Establishment of Mushroom Cultivation Center  
100 mushroom cultivation centers in selected village groups  
will be established with a fund of Rs.100 crore.  
i. Size of the mushroom cultivation center - 600 square feet.  
ii. Total expenditure for establishing one mushroom cultivation center -  
Rs.200 lakh.  
iii. Subsidy - 50% subsidy or Rs.100 lakh per unit  
will be provided.  
iv. Only one unit can be subsidized per elephant frequently.  
v. Priority will be given to women, Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe  
individuals.  
vi. The concerned district horticulture joint / deputy director,  
elephant trainer, horticulture joint director,  
treasurer (in the field of external economics, department, horticulture foreign
