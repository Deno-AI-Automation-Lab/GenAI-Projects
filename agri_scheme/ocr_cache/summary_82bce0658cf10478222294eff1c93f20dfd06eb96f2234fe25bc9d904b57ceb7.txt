**KEY FACTS - Kalaignar’s All Village Integrated Agriculture Development Programme (KAVIADP) 2022-2023**

- **G.O. Number and Date; Issuing Department:**  
  Government Order (No) No. 298 Date: 16.12.2022; Agriculture - Farmer Welfare (Division) Department

- **Scheme Name and Year; Purpose / Objective:**  
  Kalaignar’s All Village Integrated Agriculture Development Programme - 2022-2023; To implement integrated agricultural development in selected village clusters.

- **Total Budget / Sanction Amount and Funding Share (State / Central):**  
  Total Budget: Rs. 234.22926 crore; State and Central funding details not specified.

  - **Rates and Limits:** Not specified.

- **Eligibility / Beneficiaries / Priority Groups; Districts or Area Covered:**  
  Eligibility includes farmers in 3,204 village panchayats across Tamil Nadu.

  Implementing agencies include the Agriculture Department, Horticulture Department, and various other departments. Application procedures and specific conditions are not detailed in the text. Timelines for implementation are set for the financial year 2022-2023.
