Announcement No.35

Revival of Traditional Varieties in Horticulture Crops:

"Traditional varieties with Special qualities such as Kannadi kathari, Aandarkulam Kathari, Kottampatti kathari, Vari kathari, Vaasudevanallur kathari in Brinjal, Pallapatti Then murungai, Karumbu murungai in Moringa, Kuzhi thakkali, Kodi thakkali, Annanji thakkali in Tomato, Aanaikomban, Sivappu vendai in Bhendi, etc, will be motivated to cultivate in farmers field. The required vegetable seeds will be produced in State Horticultural Farms and distributed to farmers and public, who wish to grow traditional varieties in the home-stead gardens”.

Demand Announcement No: 10

Cashew Development mission

“To meet the increasing demand of cashew in national and international market, it has been proposed to increase the cashew area cultivation by providing inputs with an outlay of Rs.240 Lakh under Union and State Government fund at an area of 1500 Hectare”.

Demand Announcement no: 11

Onion Development Programme

“The price of Onion raises every year in October/ December due to decline in Onion production during the period. To bridge this gap, Department of Horticulture will provide assistance to increase the area and production of Onion to 27,170 acres at an outlay of Rs.27.74 Crore by providing assistance of Rs.8,000/acre by producing seeds and inputs. In addition to this, an assistance of Rs.18.26 Crore will be provided form establishment of Low-cost Onion storage structures in the farmer's field to store the harvested onions and market them when the demand arises”.
