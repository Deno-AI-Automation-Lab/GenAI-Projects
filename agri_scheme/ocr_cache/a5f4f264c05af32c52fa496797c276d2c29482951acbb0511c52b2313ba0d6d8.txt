From the Under Secretary to Government of India, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (Horticulture Division) letter F. No. 33-20/2021-MIDH(AAP), dated 26.05.2022.

**Order:**

In the letter second read above, the Government of India had approved the Annual Action Plan 2022-23 of National Horticulture Mission (NHM) under Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH) for an outlay of Rs.170.796 crore in the funding pattern of 60:40 between the Centre and State and communicated the approved physical and financial targets for each component under Annual Action Plan -2022-23.  

In the letters third read above, the Government of India have released 1st installment of Rs.2560.000 lakh towards implementation of the National Horticulture Mission as per the approved Annual Action Plan - 2022-23 under Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture.
