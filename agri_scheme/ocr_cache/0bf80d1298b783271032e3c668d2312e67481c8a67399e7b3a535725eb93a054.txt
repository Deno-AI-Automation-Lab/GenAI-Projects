1. **Tamil Text:**
   - G.O.(Ms) No.207
   - G.O. (Ms) No. 231 Agriculture (WD2) Department, Dated: 01.10.2019.
   - ORDER:
   - In Government Order first read above, the Government accorded administrative sanction for Rs.6567.746 lakh and financial sanction for Rs.2673.74 lakh for implementation of horticultural activities of phase II (first year) for the year 2019-20, in 16 sub basins covering 17 districts under Tamil Nadu Irrigated Agriculture Modernization Project (TNIAMP).
   - In the letter second read above, the Director of Horticulture and Plantation Crops has stated that the scheme was implemented successfully during 2019-20 as a first year programme and also stated that MD:U directed to get the total financial sanction for the remaining project periods for Phase II implementation during the year 2020-21, 2021-22 and 2022-23 and requested for the financial sanction of Tamil Nadu Irrigated Agriculture Modernization Project (TNIAMP) activities implementation under phase II second year, third year and fourth year in 16 sub basins covering 17 districts for an amount of Rs.1610.664 lakh, Rs.1253.662 lakh and Rs.1029.680 lakh respectively from the already sanctioned (Administrative sanction) amount of Rs.6567.746 lakh.
