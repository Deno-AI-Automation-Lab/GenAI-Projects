ENGLISH TRANSLATION:

6. Implementation and Structuring of the Scheme  
   i. All agreements must be properly structured,  
   ii. Based on ethnicity, new group-based migrant criteria  
       must be structured within the jurisdiction of the authority.  
   iii. Beneficiaries' feedback should be taken and new periodic intervals should be  
       established and maintained at the regional criteria level.  

III) Guidelines for the Agricultural Engineering Department's Scheme Based on Ethnicity  

1. Creation of Water Resources for Farmer Groups:  
   i. Through the Agriculture / Horticulture Departments,  
      water resources should be created in the packages.  
   ii. In the packages created by the Agriculture / Horticulture Departments,  
       the sizes of water bodies / ponds / tanks,  
       through fish nutrition / organic nutrition, natural pumps / pump systems  
       should be established and water distribution from the fish-related areas  
       should be carried out through proper distribution guidelines.  

Groundwater Survey:  
   i. To select the correct location with groundwater resources,  
      with the help of Agricultural Engineering Department's geologists/  
      Tamil Nadu Water Supply and Drainage Board's geologists/ district  
      land and development groundwater resource data center,  
      groundwater survey will be conducted through geologists or private companies.  
   ii. Based on the groundwater survey, depending on the soil characteristics,  
       decisions will be made to establish wells/tank wells/ deep wells.  

Establishment of Borewell/Tank Well  
   i. According to the groundwater survey, borewell/tank well  
      will be established.  
   ii. For community lands, borewell/tank well establishment  
       should be done for horticulture departments / in the coded package  
       as required.
