**KEY FACTS - Tamil Nadu Irrigated Agriculture Modernisation Project (TNIAMP) 2020-21**

- **G.O. number and date; issuing department:** G.O.(Ms) No.207, Dated: 04.09.2020; Agriculture (WD2) Department
- **Scheme name and year; purpose / objective:** Tamil Nadu Irrigated Agriculture Modernization Project (TNIAMP) Phase II - Second Year 2020-21; to implement horticultural activities and improve crop productivity.
- **Total budget / sanction amount and funding share (State / Central):** Total budget Rs. 6567.746 lakh; Financial sanction Rs. 1610.664 lakh for 2020-21.
- **Components with subsidy pattern, unit cost, rates and limits:**
  - Crop incentive scheme: Rs. 2500/ha as cultivation assistance.
  - Individual Based Subsidy: Rs. 70000.
- **Eligibility / beneficiaries / priority groups; districts or area covered:** Farmers in 16 sub basins covering 17 districts.
- **Implementing agencies, how to apply, conditions, timelines:** Implemented by the Director of Horticulture and Plantation Crops; application details not specified; conditions include achieving targets without shortfall; timelines for financial sanctions for subsequent years are 2021-22 and 2022-23.
