ENGLISH TRANSLATION:
As mentioned in Government Order 3, the allocation of funds designated for General (80%), Scheduled Castes (19%), and Scheduled Tribes (1%) is to be reviewed for the new period regarding the fund distribution procedure. District Collectors and officials are instructed to implement the allocation of 80% of the funds to the departments of Arts and Crafts, and the Vocal Music Scheme from the year 2024-25 in the 2482 villages undertaken, and the remaining 20% is to be implemented in villages according to the procedural guidelines for the new period.
Furthermore, without any delay, the new period's scheme should be implemented. The date certificate and beneficiary details should be submitted to this office.
