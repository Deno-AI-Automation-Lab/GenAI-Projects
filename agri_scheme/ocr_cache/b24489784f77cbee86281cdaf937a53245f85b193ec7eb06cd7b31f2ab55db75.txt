As a new initiative, crop incentive scheme is proposed to be introduced in the Tamil Nadu Irrigated Agriculture Modernization Project (TNIAMP) component of crop diversification in vegetables. In order to encourage impact area farmers an incentive amount of Rs.2500/ha will be given as a cultivation assistance. This will increase more impact area and thereby vegetable production also increased without exceeding overall budget.

3. In this connection, the Director of Horticulture and Plantation Crops has requested the Government to accord orders on the following issues:-

(i) To accord financial sanction of Rs. 1610.664 lakh, Rs.1253.662 lakh and Rs.1029.680 lakh towards the implementation of phase II second year, third year and fourth year TNIAMP activities during 2020-21, 2021-2022 and 2022-23 respectively in 16 sub basins covering 17 districts according to the administrative sanction of Rs.6567.746 lakh for the entire phase II TNIAMP activities implementation vide G.O. (Ms) No. 231 Agriculture (WD2) Department, Dated:01.10.2019.

(ii) To Permit the Director of Horticulture and Plantation Crops to interchange the component and also cost norms without altering/exceeding the total financial outlay. The Director of Horticulture and Plantation Crops shall increase and decrease the target and funds based on the potential and need of the sub basins.

(iii) To permit the Director of Horticulture and Plantation Crops to appoint Technical Input Providers-10 posts as per contract basis.
